# SONALZA legal launch checklist

This checklist is operational guidance, not a substitute for legal advice.

## Before enabling paid orders in Mexico
- Publish the legal identity of the operator/responsible party.
- Publish a physical business address suitable for consumer/privacy notices.
- Publish a customer-support telephone number.
- Confirm the final business structure and invoicing/tax workflow with the CPA.
- Confirm the Mexico privacy notice identifies the responsible party and address.

## Before Stripe LIVE
- Keep Terms and Privacy links visible before checkout.
- Store Terms acceptance, Privacy acceptance, materials authorization, legal versions, timestamp, order ID, and payment ID.
- Make the final amount, currency, taxes/fees, and delivery method visible before payment.
- Keep the customer-facing checkout free of internal references to Supabase, Resend, Vercel, or test infrastructure.
- Test refund and dispute workflows.

## Music/IP
- Personal-song purchases default to personal-use licenses only.
- Do not promise full copyright ownership or registrability.
- Require a written commercial license before DSP commercial release, paid ads, Content ID, brand campaigns, synchronization, sublicensing, or resale.
- Do not include stems, project files, prompts, or production source files unless the proposal explicitly says so.
- Keep Business license scope in writing: media, territory, term, exclusivity, deliverables, and restrictions.

## Privacy / security
- Keep the custom-cover storage bucket private.
- Do not store full payment-card data.
- Maintain a practical deletion/retention procedure for uploaded photos and creative source materials.
- Keep marketing consent separate from transactional messages.
- If analytics/ads become non-essential or cross-site, review whether a cookie-consent manager is required before enabling them.

## Territories
- Initial consumer checkout should be limited to United States and Mexico.
- Do not market SONALZA as “available worldwide” until country-specific legal/privacy/tax requirements are reviewed.
- If later targeting the EEA/UK, Canada, Brazil, or other markets, update privacy notices and consent/consumer-law controls before launch in those territories.

## Before public launch
- Have a California e-commerce/IP attorney review the final Terms and license model.
- Have Mexico consumer/privacy compliance reviewed before paid Mexico orders.
- Re-run mobile/desktop tests in Spanish and English.
- Test a US order and a Mexico order end-to-end.
